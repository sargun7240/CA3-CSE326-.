1. Problem Statement & Text Formatting

Why This Platform Is Needed

Conservation teams collect observations about marine species and environmental conditions from different locations. Without structured records, organizing these observations is difficult.

Our project helps record and explore marine conservation observations such as sightings, locations, dates, environmental conditions, photographs, and notes.

Text formatting examples: Bold text, Strong importance, Italic text, Emphasized text, Highlighted observation note, Small side note for students, Old paper logbook, New digital record, Water formula H2O, Temperature exponent 252 scale note, and short quote Protect our oceans every day.

Structured marine observation records help conservation teams track species and environmental changes across coastal regions.

Citation example: Coastal Wildlife Field Guide. Abbreviation example: MPA. Definition example: Habitat is the natural home of an organism. Code example: recordObservation(). Preformatted text example: 

Obs ID   | Species            | Condition
OBS-101  | Olive Ridley       | Optimal
OBS-102  | Staghorn Coral     | Critical

2. Observation Lists & Guidelines

Steps to Record an Observation (Ordered List)

  1. Choose the marine species and location.
  2. Check the date and environmental condition:
    1. Clear or turbid water
    2. Temperature and weather
  3. Add notes and save the record.

Monitored Marine Items (Unordered List)

Key Conservation Terms (Description List)

Species Record
Details of the marine organism observed at a location.
Environmental Condition
Status of water and surrounding marine environment such as Optimal, Moderate, or Critical.
Search / Filter
Finding specific marine observations quickly by keyword or condition.
